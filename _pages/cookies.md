*Last updated: October 2026*

## This site does not use cookies

marcobalduini.com does not set cookies, and does not use similar technologies such as local storage, to recognise or track visitors: no technical, analytics, profiling or advertising cookies. For this reason no cookie banner is shown.

## Third-party services

- **Visit statistics.** [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/) counts visits without cookies and without identifying individual visitors.
- **Hosting and icons.** GitHub Pages (hosting) and jsDelivr (icons) receive your IP address to deliver the content, but do not set cookies on this site.

More details are in the [privacy policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}).

## External sites

When you follow a link to another website (for example LinkedIn, GitHub or a publisher), that website may use its own cookies under its own policy.

## Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings. Since this site does not use cookies, doing so does not affect how it works.
