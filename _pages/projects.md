## Funded research
{: #funded-research}

### TRIAD
*Horizon Europe, 2026–*

"Balancing Foundational and AI Competences for Inclusive, Well-being-Oriented Education". I lead WP7 (Data Enrichment, Integration and Exploratory Dashboard): consolidating pilot data and enriching it with external datasets, building an interactive web dashboard, and publishing APIs and metadata following FAIR and Frictionless Data principles.

### AIS4UM — AI-Safety 4 Smart Urban Mobility
*Regione Lombardia, 2025–*

I am responsible for data management, integration and analysis and coordinate technical work across partners: a data catalogue of sources for the project use cases, harmonization of geographic data such as the Milan road graph, and a data strategy built on static and open sources to keep use cases independent from partners' real-time feeds.

### Equal-Life
*Horizon 2020, 2020–2025*

European project on how physical and social environments shape children's mental health and development, coordinated by RIVM. I led the design and development of the Equal-Life toolbox — an exploratory tool and a semantic knowledge tool that make the project's metadata, documents, models, protocols and code navigable — refined through co-design workshops with stakeholders across Europe.

[Project website](https://www.equal-life.eu/en){: .btn .btn--small}

## Past projects

Augmented-reality mobile app for tourists in Seoul powered by semantic, continuous and predictive social media analysis, co-designed by Politecnico di Milano, Siemens and Saltlux. Winner of the Semantic Web Challenge 2011.

### CitySensing
*2014–2015*

Visual dashboards fusing social media, mobile telecommunication and IoT data, co-developed with the DensityDesign Lab of Politecnico di Milano and Telecom Italia; exhibited at Milan Design Week 2014 and EXPO Milano 2015. IEEE MultiMedia Best Paper Award 2016.

### Fluxedo
*2015*

Co-founded startup for continuous visual analytics; its first social media monitoring product was built on the heterogeneous stream processing engine from my PhD.
